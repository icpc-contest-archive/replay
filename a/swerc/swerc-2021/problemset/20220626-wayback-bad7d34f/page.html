Training

You can submit your solutions to the problems on codeforces. They will be evaluated on the same test data which was used in the official contest, with the same time limit.

If you want to train by virtual participating in this contest, you shall virtual participate in the mirror contest hosted on codeforces. Notice that the mirror contains three additional (hard) problems which were not present in the official contest.
