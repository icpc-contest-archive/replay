By ismailfateen, 14 months ago,

Hello codeforces!

ECPC (Egyptian Collegiate Programming Contest) 2025 has just concluded so I decided to post a blog with the teams qualified to ACPC (Africa and Arab Collegiate Programming Contest) 2025. ACPC will be held in the beginning of December.

Huge congratulations to "Tamora fel d2e2a 90" from Cairo University — Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence for winning ECPC 2025!!

If your team qualified, kindly put your Country, University (and College if applicable), teamname and your teammates' cf profiles and I will add it to the list.

Best of luck in ACPC!
