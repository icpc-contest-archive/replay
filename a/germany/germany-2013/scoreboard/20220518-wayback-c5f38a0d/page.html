GCPC 2013

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2013 was held on June, 15th. The winner was team hacKIT from KIT solving 8 problems (out of 11) in a total time of 1063. It was a again close and exciting competition with many solved problems in the last hour, where four more universities entering the top ten (LMU, FAU, TUM, Lübeck).

You may be interested in the problem set, the sample input, the secret test data, the solution outlines and some judge solutions.
