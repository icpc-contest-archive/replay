GCPC 2020

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2020 was held completely online on November, 21th. 68 teams competed allover Germany. The winner was team Dolores TUMbridge from TU München, with all 13 problems solved in a total time of 1720 minutes.

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret + jury submissions).
