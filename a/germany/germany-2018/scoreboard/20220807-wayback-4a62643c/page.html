GCPC 2018

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2018 was held on June, 16th. 123 teams competed at 11 sites allover Germany. The winner was team vO)> from UdS, with 12 solved problems (out of 13) in a total time of 1725 minutes. Eight different universities entered the top ten (UdS, KIT, TUM, Ulm, LMU, Kaiserslautern, Lübeck, FAU).

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret), and the jury solutions.
