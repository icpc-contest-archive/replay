GCPC 2011

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2011 was held on July, 2nd. The winner was team segFAUlt from FAU solving 9 problems (out of 10) in a total time of 1677. It was a close and exciting competition with many solved problems in the last hour, where Blamage a Trois from Rostock could not hold their top position.

You may be interested in the problem set, the sample input (missing), the secret test data, and the solution outlines.
