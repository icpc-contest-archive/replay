GCPC 2016

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2016 was held on June, 4th. 96 teams competed at several sites allover Germany. The winner was team breaKIT from KIT, with 10 solved problems (out of 12) in a total time of 941 minutes. Four different universities entered the top ten (KIT, UDS, TUM, FAU).

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret), and the jury solutions (missing).
