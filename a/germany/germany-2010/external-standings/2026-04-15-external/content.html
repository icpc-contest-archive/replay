German Collegiate Programming Contest

German Collegiate Programming Contest 2010

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2010 was held on June, 12th. The winner was team deFAUlt from FAU solving 10 problems (out of 11) in a total time of 1447. It was a close and exciting competition with the result of teams from 7 different universities in the top 10.

You may be interested in the problem set, the sample input, the secret test data and the solution outlines.
