GCPC 2012

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2012 was held on June, 30th. The winner was team easy cheesy peasy from FAU solving 10 problems (out of 11) in a total time of 1478. It was a close and exciting competition with many solved problems in the last hour, where five more universities entering the top ten (LMU, KIT, Rostock, TUM, Saarland).

You may be interested in the problem set, the sample input, the secret test data, the solution outlines and some judge solutions.
