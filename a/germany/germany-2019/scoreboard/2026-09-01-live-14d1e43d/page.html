GCPC 2019

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2019 was held on July, 6th. 94 teams competed at 11 sites allover Germany. The winner was team TUMbledor from TU München, with all 13 problems solved in a total time of 1526 minutes.

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret + jury submissions).
