GCPC 2014

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2014 was held on May, 24th. 64 teams competed at several sites allover Germany. The winner was team JustKITing from KIT, with 8 solved problems (out of 11) in a total time of 1100 minutes. It was a again close and exciting competition with many solved problems in the last hour, where four more universities entering the top ten (Göttingen, FAU, Saarland, TUM).

You may be interested in the problem set, test data (sample + secret, missing), and the solution outlines.
