GCPC 2021

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2021 was held completely online on June, 26th. 85 teams competed allover Germany. The winner was team Dolores TUMbridge from TU München, with 12 problems solved in a total time of 1137 minutes. Seven different universities entered the top ten (TUM, KIT, HPI, Saarland, Ulm, CAU, FAU).

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret + jury submissions).
