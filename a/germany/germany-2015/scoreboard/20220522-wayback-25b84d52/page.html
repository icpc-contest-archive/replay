GCPC 2015

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2015 was held on June, 20th. 62 teams competed at several sites allover Germany. The winner was team oachkatzlschwoaf++ from TUM, with 7 solved problems (out of 11) in a total time of 988 minutes. It was a close and exciting race since both teams with seven problems (oachkatzlschwoaf++ from TUM and FAUgrau from FAU) solved a problem in the last twenty minutes. Six different universities entered the top ten (TUM, FAU, LMU, Göttingen, KIT, Mainz).

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret), and the jury solutions.
