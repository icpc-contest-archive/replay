GCPC 2017

The German Collegiate Programming Contest 2017 was held on July, 1st. 128 teams competed at several sites allover Germany. The winner was team Hello KITty 😼 from KIT, with 10 solved problems (out of 11) in a total time of 1339 minutes. Six different universities entered the top ten (KIT, TUM, Saarland, FAU, Ulm, HPI).

You may be interested in the problem set and the solution outlines, test data (sample + secret), and the jury solutions.
