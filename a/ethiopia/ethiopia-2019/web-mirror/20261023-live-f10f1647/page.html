📢 CONTEST DAY SCHEDULE & IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Dear Contestants and Coaches,
Please take note of the following schedule and important guidelines for the EtCPC 2026 contest day.

🕒 Schedule
3:00 LT in the morning — Arrival at the ICT Center
Before the contest — Group photo in front of the ICT Center
4:00 LT in the morning sharp — Main contest begins
9:00 LT in the afternoon — Main contest ends
After the contest — Dinner break for contestants
At night — Closing ceremony at Room B508, Block 9 (specific time will be announced)

🍪 Snacks: Contestants are allowed to bring simple snacks into the contest halls.
📄 Cheat Sheets: Contestants are allowed to bring a cheat sheet of up to 25 pages maximum.

🤝 We encourage all teams and coaches to follow the schedule and instructions to ensure a smooth and successful contest.
