Registrations are open!

Welcome to the 2002 ACM South American Regional Programming Contest. This year 7 sites will hold simultaneous contests on November 8-9, in order to select the teams which will advance to the 2002 ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest Finals in Hollywood, U.S.A.
