Buckle up for the first official National Programming Contest of Turkey!September 13-15
By inzva, history, 7 years ago,

inzva is organizing the first official ‘Turkish Programming Contest’ ever; which will be held on September 13-15, 2019 at Beykoz Kundura in Istanbul.

This first-of-its-kind event is a multi-tier, team-based programming competition that will be operated under the auspices of the International Collegiate Programming Contest (“ICPC”), and partnered with Middle East Technical University.

PRIZE

The top ten teams qualifying in this contest can then participate in the Southeastern Europe Regional Contest (‘‘SEERC’’) held in Bucharest on October 18 to 20, and will be financially supported for this purpose.

REGISTRATION

Please go to Team Registration to register your teams on the official ICPC system. This step is necessary in order to participate in the contest.

To complete your registration;

1- Login or register with ICPC online 2- Make one team registration, providing the team’s and member’s names. 3- If you come across any issues, contact ICPC online.

Registration will end at 19:00 on August 31, 2019 (Saturday).

VENUE

Turkish Programming Contest 2019 will be held on September 13-15, 2019 at Beykoz Kundura in Istanbul.

If you have any questions, please feel free to send an email to [email protected].

All participants have to abide by our CODE OF CONDUCT.

Good luck and have fun!

Attention: The Grand Contest 2019 starts on June 22, 2019 at 10:00 am!
By inzva, 7 years ago,

Attention: The Grand Contest 2019 starts on June 22, 2019 at 10:00 am!

This is the first international programming contest organized to take place on Saturday, June 22, 2019 at 10:00 am (UTC + 3), by inzva and in collaboration with algotester.com.

We are inzva, a non-profit organization based in Istanbul, Turkey; bringing Turkish computer science students, AI and Algorithm enthusiasts and academics together.

We invite you to test yourself and your team at the Grand Contest on Saturday, June 22 at 10:00 am.

20 best teams will get prize t-shirts for each team member. The contest duration is 5 hours. The allowed programming languages are C/C++, Pascal, Java, C# and Python.

The Grand Contest is an ICPC-oriented contest in which experienced university students will have the opportunity to test their skill level in teams, and prepare for international contests. It will be onsite at places such as Istanbul, Lviv, Belgrade and Minsk; as well as taking place online.

The round will be held according to the rules of ICPC. Please note that you must be in teams of two or three students. Do not forget to register for the contest..The registration will be closed 5 minutes before the contest.

If you have any questions, please feel free to send an email to [email protected].

Good luck and have fun!

Algorithm Competition Summer Camp 2019 in Istanbul — June 16-25 — Div. A.

By inzva, 7 years ago,

We are inzva; a non-profit organisation based in Istanbul, Turkey, that brings Turkish computer science students, AI and Algorithm enthusiasts and academics together.

We invite you to test yourself and your team to join the Expert division of Algorithm Competition Summer Camp 2019 between June 16-25.

Expert Group is an ICPC-oriented division of ACSC ‘19, in which experienced university students will have the opportunity to raise their skill level in teams, work with international students and prepare for international contests through practices, lectures, and problem-solving sessions. There will be 10 or more teams in Expert Group and the discussions will be carried out in English.

The coaches: -Vitalii Herasymiv https://codeforces.com/profile/witua?locale=en http://myicpc.live/World-Finals-2019/people/819

-Andrii Makar https://codeforces.com/profile/Andrew_Makar http://myicpc.live/World-Finals-2019/people/3243

To join the Expert Group and take part in ACSC, you must first fill in the application form here, where you can get further information about the camp.

Please note that you must be in teams of two or three students from the same university. There will be an Eligibility Round to make sure that the participants have the necessary skills, which will be held on** May 10-11**. All events of inzva are free of charge. All instructions will be delivered to applicants via email.
