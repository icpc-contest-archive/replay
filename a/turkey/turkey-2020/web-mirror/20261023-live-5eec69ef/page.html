  Online Programming Contest for Universities

Start
End

inzva greets the winter of 2020 with an online programming contest designed for all universities across Turkey and invites university teams to participate in the contest, completely free of charge.

Online Programming Contest for Universities (“OPC”) is a two-round, team-based contest that will be held on December 12 and December 26, respectively, and is initiated in collaboration with leading Turkish universities and algotester.com.

SCHEDULE

  • Application Period: October 24 - December 5, 2020 until 19:00
  • Practical Session: Friday, December 7, 2020 at 19:00
  • Online 'Qualification' Round: Saturday, December 12, 2020 between 11:00 - 16:00
  • Announcement of Finalist Teams: Wednesday, December 16
  • Online Final Contest: Saturday, December 26, 2020 between 11:00 - 16:00
  • Closing Ceremony: Saturday, December 26, 2020 at 18:00

PRIZE

  • Tech gifts for the top five teams at Final Contest
  • inzva kit for the teams that place between the top six and top ten at Final Contest

RULES

  • Participating teams must consist of three students who are enrolled in the same university
  • The participants must send the answers of the problems from only one team account. Team account information should be the same as the registration account information
  • All team members must follow the schedule and provide full participation
  • Teams or members who fail to follow the rules will be disqualified
  • The contest will follow the official ICPC rules. Please read them carefully
  • The contest will last 5 hours and consist of problems prepared by professional coaches in the algotester team
  • If the team decides to change/remove team members, they should inform [email protected] of the said changes
  • In case of plagiarism, the fact that it was an individual and/or collective effort does not matter. The teams committing plagiarism will be disqualified, and they will be banned from the next Turkish Programming Contest 2021
