Bilkent University Excels in inzva’s Online Programming Contest 2023

Bilkent University Computer Engineering Department continues to showcase its prowess in competitive programming, with 3 of its teams securing top 10 positions in the 4th Annual Online Programming Contest for Universities (OPC) organized by inzva. The contest, held on November 4, saw a total of 122 teams from 26 different universities across Turkey participating, to foster algorithmic thinking, enhancing analytical skills, and promoting teamwork among computer engineering students.

8 of the 78 teams actively engaged in the competition are hailed from Bilkent University. Impressively, Bilkent’s teams secured 3 spots, including the 2nd in the contest, in the top 10.

The 3 teams from the top 10 are:
– Ahelsa, 2nd
– 2Fast1Curious, 6th
– Musebu, 10th

For a detailed look at the leaderboard and overall standings, you can visit Algoleague’s official website.
