2014 Rocky Mountain Regional ACM Contest Information

Thanks to everyone who participated or helped with this years contest!

Check out the final standings by rank. The problem set is now available as a zip file. The zip file contains statements, slides about the solutions, and data sets.

GENERAL INFORMATION

The 2014 Rocky Mountain Regional Contest draws students from colleges and universities throughout Arizona, Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, Eastern Nevada, Idaho, Montana, Alberta, Saskatchewan, New Mexico (excluding Dona Ana county).

Winners selected from regional contests worldwide will advance to the ACM-ICPC World Finals to compete for bragging rights and scholarships.
