2007 Rocky Mountain Regional ACM Contest Information

FINAL STANDINGS

Thanks to everyone who participated or helped with this years contest!

Check out the final standings by rank and by team. The problem set is available as a zip file. The zip file contains statements, data sets, and solutions.

GENERAL INFORMATION

The 2006 Rocky Mountain Regional Contest draws students from colleges and universities throughout Arizona, Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, Eastern Nevada, Idaho, Montana, Alberta, Saskatchewan, New Mexico (excluding Dona Ana county).

Winners selected from regional contests worldwide will advance to the 2007 ACM Programming Contest World Finals to compete for bragging rights and scholarships.
