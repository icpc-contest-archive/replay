Pacific NW Region

Welcome to the Pacific Region Programming Contest! The Pacific Region is comprised of the following areas: Alaska, British Columbia, Washington, Oregon, northern/central California and western Nevada. Because of the large geographic area of the region, the Pacific Contest is held simultaneously at two sites: one in California, and one in Washington or Oregon. Regional contests are held each Fall, and qualifying teams are then eligible to compete in the World Finals, held the following Spring.
