Team Roster

Why is my team in pending state?

First and second teams will be able to take part in the contest, when all member contestants are eligible to take part. We are still checking eligibility.
We are sorry, but we could only accept two teams per university, due to limited space.
Please also note that a team must consist of three team members. There will not be any exception to this rule.
So the reason that your team is still in pending state is a forgotten birth date, or incomplete registration data, which is all currently being resolved. This list is ordered by team name.
