Making our future

University of Bath Strategy 2036

Our 10-year strategy to transform the world through creating new knowledge, shared and delivered through our education, research and innovation.

Our strategy sets out our overarching framework for success, which we are underpinning with detailed implementation plans.

Our strategic areas of focus are set out in three layers:

  • Global excellence with impact
  • Building from our strengths
  • Foundations for the future
View our strategy

Our new visual identity

As we embark on the next chapter for our University, our visual identity is evolving with us. This reflects our ambition and commitment to global excellence with impact, as well as our deep connection with the City of Bath, our history and heritage.
