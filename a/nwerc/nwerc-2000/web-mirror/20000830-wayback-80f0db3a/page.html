Welcome to the North Western Regional Programming Contest 2000 at Darmstadt University of Technology. You are viewing the official webpage of the regional contest which will be held from November 17th to 19th 2000 at the department of computer science in Darmstadt, Germany.

The contest is organized by the Association for Computing Machinery for students worldwide. Last year 1968 teams from 1041 universities in 69 countries participated in 42 regional contests at 82 sites, from which 60 teams advanced to the world finals, which were held in Orlando, Florida, USA.

You will hopefully find all information you need to participate on this site. If you miss anything or have questions, contact the Regional Contest Director of this contest. We hope to see you on November 17th.
