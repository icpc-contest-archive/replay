Welcome to the Northwestern Europe Regional Programming Contest 2001 at Darmstadt University of Technology. You are viewing the official webpage of the regional contest which was held from November 2nd to 4th 2001 at the department of computer science in Darmstadt, Germany.

The contest is organized by the Association for Computing Machinery for students worldwide. In 2000 2700 teams from 1079 universities in 70 countries participated in 42 regional contests at 82 sites, from which 64 teams advanced to the world finals, which were held in Vancouver, Canada.

You will hopefully find all information you need to participate on this site. If you miss anything or have questions, contact the Regional Contest Director of this contest. We hope to see you on November 2nd.
