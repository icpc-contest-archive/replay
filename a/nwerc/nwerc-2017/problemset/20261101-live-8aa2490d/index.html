Bath reaches top 125 in QS World University Rankings 2027

We are proud to have risen seven places since last year, achieving our highest ranking since the introduction of independent rankings in 2010.

Result highlights

This is the fourth consecutive year that we have featured in the QS Top 150, placing us in the top 10% of universities worldwide.

Further highlights include rankings of:

  • 56th for Sustainability
  • 91st for Employer Reputation
