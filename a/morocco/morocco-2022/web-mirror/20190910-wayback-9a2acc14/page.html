Nos élèves se distinguent : Une équipe de l'ENSIAS s'est qualifié pour l'ACPC (ACM arab Collegiate Programming Contest)

The Moroccan Collegiate Programming Contest (MCPC) is a problem solving collegiate competition that focuses on programming skills. It is a qualifying round for the ACM Arab Collegiate Programming Contest (ACPC). MCPC is organized in the same spirit and is governed by all applicable rules of the ICPC. Winners of an MCPC contest get the chance to represent Morocco in the Arab world and compete against the best Arab teams. Winning teams from the ACPC, are qualified to the final round which is the International Collegiate Programming Contest: ICPC.

Solving the competition’s problems requires a lot of hard work and dedication to cumulate enough knowledge and skills from different fields like maths, algorithms and programming.

ENSIAS was represented by 5 teams and one of them - Beginners Luck - made it to the ACM Arab Collegiate Programming Contest (ACPC) by ranking second at the competition. It is important to add that the 2018’s edition of MCPC was the first MCPC experience for all members of BeginnersLuck team.
