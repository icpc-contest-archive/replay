Hosting a Site!

Who wants to host a site for n teams? Contact Ryan Stansifer.

  • Each site can set their own limit on teams, say n=20 or n=30.
  • n networked computers and work space for each team of 3 people; 2 printers
  • Lunch for 4*n people; meeting room for instructions and results
  • volunteers: director, computers/networking, floor manager/volunteer coordinator, runners, ballons
  • Site information: campus maps, lodging information, parking, directions, signs
  • Budget: approximately $40/person from registration fees

We take care of centralized administration:

  • Registration, collecting fees, receipts
  • T-shirts, medals, plaques
  • Contest environment, flash drives
  • Contest administration, scoreboard
  • Judging
