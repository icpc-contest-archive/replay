These unofficial standings are derived from information reported by the contest sites.
Teams are listed in rank order, and alphabetically within rank.
The primary indicator of rank is the number of problems solved. Among teams that have
solved the same number of problems, teams are further ranked on the number of "penalty
points" accrued. A point is accrued for each minute from the beginning of competition
(12:30 PM CDT) to the time when the correct solution was submitted.
An additional 20 penalty points are added for each failed submission prior
to the correct submission. No points are accrued for unsolved problems.
Teams that are tied (solved the same number of problems
with the same number of penalty points) are listed in alphabetic order by school/team name.
