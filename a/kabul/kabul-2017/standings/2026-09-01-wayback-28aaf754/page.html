Standings for The 2017 ACM-ICPC Asia Kabul Regional Contest

The 2017 ACM-ICPC Asia Kabul Regional Programming Contest hosted by Kateb University on 10th November. Total of 10 teams from both Afghanistan and Iran from 6 universities participated in contest. Afghanistan teams are from Avicenna University, Gharjistan University, Kabul Polytechnic University, Kardan University, Kateb University. Also, Shahid Beheshti University from Iran participated in.


The winner of the contest is You Know Izad? from Shahid Beheshti University (Iran). Second position has taken by “KPU-CE” from Polytechnical University of Kabul. Aslo, Kateb University stands at third position.


Our special thank goes to ACM/ICPC Foundation for sponsorship and their great people who help us run the contest in Afghanistan. Interested students from Afghanistan and Iran have made our contest wonderful and memorable. We have got many new experiences from every moment of the contest. Volunteers both professors and students have done amazing activities. I would like to thanks all for what the have done.
