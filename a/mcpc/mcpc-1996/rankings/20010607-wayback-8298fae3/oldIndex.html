The ACM Mid-Central Contest Region

The Mid-Central Region, clustered around the central Mississippi River, includes Missouri, Arkansas, Kentucky, Tennessee and Illinois, states once belonging to the North Central, South Eastern and Southern Regions of the ACM Programming Contest. The Mid-Central Region each year is host to approximately 80 teams through its nine satellite locations from which emerges three teams which will be sent to the ACM Contest Finals held next year at San Jose, CA, March 1, 1997. This year the three top teams from the region are:

University of Missouri - Rolla (A)
Northwest Missouri State University (A)
University of Louisville (A)

The regional officials include a regional director and a three-member steering committee: Steven Baber, Edward Hammerand, and Ron Pacheco.

The Regional Contest is organized and run each year by the Mid-Central Regional Contest Steering Committee, chaired by the Mid-Central Regional Contest Director. The Regional Contest is coordinated by the ACM Director of North American Contests, Dr. Don Bagert, Texas Tech University, Lubbock, Tx. The Regional Contest Director is responsible for the overall planning and management of the contest. The director manages the contest effort as it moves toward the actual event. The smoothness of operation goes a long way toward reducing the frustration of teams, judges, and other volunteers. Any exceptions are administered by the director.
