2020 ICPC Results

2020 was an unusual year. At the time, the world was in the grip of the COVID-19 pandemic. We tried an experiment. Instead of having a Divisional Contest that qualified teams for a Regional Contest, we had a single large contest with both a Divisional 1 and a Division 2.

Congratulations to the teams “TLE on test case 2020” (UNSW) and “taxiOverflow” (Uni of Melb) who qualified to represent our region at the World Finals as well as all the teams who competed this year. The University of Adelaide gets a special mention for fielding the most teams. It is wonderful to see such a high level of participation.
