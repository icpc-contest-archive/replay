2014 ICPC Results

Regional Finals

The thirteen teams who qualified for the Regional Finals, gathered in Sydney on 26th September to compete for places in the next World Finals.

  • The winners are Australian National University.
  • Runners up are University of New South Wales.
  • Third place are University of Western Australia.
