2012 ICPC Results

2012 Finals – Regional Scoreboard

The 2012 South Pacific Regional Contest was held on Saturday September 18th at 11 sites across Australia and New Zealand.

The winning team was Brogrammers from the University of Sydney. They will advance to the 2013 World Finals along with The Four Milkmen from the University of New South Wales who finished 2nd.
