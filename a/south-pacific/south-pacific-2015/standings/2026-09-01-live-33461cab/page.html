2015 ICPC Results

2015 South Pacific Regional Contest

The 2015 South Pacific ICPC Regional Contest was held on Saturday, September 26, 2015, with preliminary divisional rounds held on August 23, 2015. Teams competed across three geographic divisions before the top qualifiers advanced to the Regional Final.

Regional Final Results

12 teams from across Australia and New Zealand competed in the Regional Final at AUT’s Tech Central. The contest featured 11 problems (A-K).
