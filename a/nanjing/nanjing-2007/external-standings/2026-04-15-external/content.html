*Note: The official ranks are based on the Asia-Specific Rules and Nanjing Site Rules, where the ranking is based on the top team from each university/college. Specifically, final standings will first be ranked from the top team from each university/college. Other teams from the same college will then be ranked as ties with that of the next ranked team of another university. If a university/college has a champion team in other regional site, other teams from the same university/college and informal team will be also will then be ranked as ties with that of the next ranked team of another university.
