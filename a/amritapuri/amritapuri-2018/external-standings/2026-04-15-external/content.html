Qualified Team for ICPC Amritapuri Regional Contest 2021

Eligibility

Students who are currently enrolled in a university are eligible. Additionally, students who got graduated in 2021 & those who are graduating in 2022 are eligible to participate for the ICPC 2021 contest.

Registration fees per team, to participate in the online round is INR 600 /-.

Steps for online contest registration

  • Each team shall have a coach. Coach is the faculty member from the same Institution/University/College.
  • Coach creates a team in the ICPC Global Site under “Asia Amritapuri First Round Online Programming Contest” Online contest.
  • Coach is expected to create a team with the official email id. Official email id means, he/she shall use the institutional email id. While creating teams, the coach shall invite the contestants and reserve.
  • A team consists of at most 3 contestants and the Team members (contestants) shall complete their profile in ICPC Global Site. It is mandatory that all the details as required in the system should be completed before the last date.
  • The team shall pay the registration fees.

How to Pay Registration Fee

How to get the previous year certificate?

Once the payment is received your status will be updated on our Google sheet and accepted on the ICPC Global site.

What is the role of reserved person? Will the reserved person get the certificate?

In case of any emergency to the team in the absence of any person the reserved can participate. Yes, the reserved person will get the certificate.

What is the role of a Coach?
  • Coach is the faculty member from the same college as that of the team.
  • Coach creates a team in the ICPC Global Site under “Asia Amritapuri First Round Online Programming Contest” Online contest.
  • Coach is expected to create a team with the official email id. Official email id means, he/she shall use the institutional email id. While creating teams, the coach shall invite the contestants and reserve.
How many members should be in a team? Is it mandatory to keep a reserve person?

There should be three members in a team, including reserve person four members. It is not mandatory to keep a reserve person.
