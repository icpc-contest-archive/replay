The 2015 ACM-ICPC Asia Phuket Regional Contest Results

After 5 hours of competition, the contestants could solve almost 12 contest problems except 2 (problem J and K). In a few minutes at the beginning of contest, 3 problems were already solved by 3 fastest teams: Problem L was first solved at 3 minutes by team “CUCP Meow Meow :3” from Chulalongkorn University (Thailand) , Problem A was first solved at 3 minutes by team “NCTU_Railgun” from National Chiao Tung University (Taiwan) and Problem B was first solved at 6 minutes by team “CUCP Too Much Pain” from Chulalongkorn University (Thailand).
