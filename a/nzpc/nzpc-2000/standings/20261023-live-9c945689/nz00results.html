Auckland University had a strong showing in the recent New Zealand 
Programming Contest (Aug 5th) by having the overall top team.  Our stage 
two team actually came in 3rd overall and we had a stage one team that 
tied in top points with a stage one team competing at the Hamilton site.

The 1st place team consisted of Po Shao (Parker Shaw), Lev Gimel'farb 
and Vane Trajkov.  Our 3rd place team (stage two winners) consisted of 
Benjamin Whale and Doug Wilson.  Auckland's top stage 1 team consisted of 
Chris Wilson, Tom Leys and Simon Waterer.
