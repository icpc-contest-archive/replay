2001 ACM South Africa Programming Contest
sponsored by
IBM
hosted by
Department of Computer Science, University of Pretoria, South Africa
Additional location(s): Western Cape - University of Cape Town
Registration
Localisation of the contest rules

Date of Contest: 15 September 2001

The contest draws students from colleges and universities throughout southern Africa.

Winners selected from regional contests on six continents will advance to the ACM Programming Contest World Finals in Honolulu!, to compete for bragging rights and scholarships.

To keep up to date with the planning, please subscribe to this web page or visit it regularly. The official web pages of the International Contest are at http://acm.baylor.edu/acmicpc/. There, you will find links to:

  • The rules, including: your team's composition, the structure of the contest, conduct at the contest, programming languages used, and mandatory attendance at some of the contest-day functions.
  • The registration system, which will allow your team coach to register for the contest and obtain updated information. In addition to registering through the system, please email the Regional Contest Director as well.
