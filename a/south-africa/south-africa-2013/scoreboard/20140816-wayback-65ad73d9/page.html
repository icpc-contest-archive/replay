South Africa (2013)

The 2013 contest will take place on 12 October 2013 at the following sites in South Africa:
Pretoria, Cape Town, Grahamstown, Pietermaritzburg.
A site in Benin as well as Nigeria for West African contestants. A pilot site will be open in Kenya for East African contestants.
