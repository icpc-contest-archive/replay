Report

The eighth ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest in the South African region took place on Saturday 14 October 2006. There were 49 entries at 4 sites. University of Cape Town (UCT) played host to teams from UCT, Stellenbosch University (SU) and the University of the Western Cape (UWC). Rhodes university (RU) participated in Grahamstown and University of KwaZulu-Natal (KZN) in Pietermaritzburg (KZN). Finally, the University of Pretoria (UP) hosted teams from UP, the University of the Witwatersrand (WITS) and Tshwane University of Technology (TUT).

The University of Cape Town's Traveling Salesman took top hounours by completing 5 problems in the alloted 5 hours. Second, Third and Fourth places all managed to complete 4 problems, meaning that the placement was dependent on the time it took to complete the problems.
